# The Angels' Wing website archive

The Angels' Wing was a virtual 3D gallery and community for independent NFT artists, running from March 2022 to September 2023.

This is the archive. One static page holds the story, all 33 artists with their bios, portraits and artwork, the floor plan of the exhibition, the video index and Ping's music credit. Alongside it sit five guides on buying and judging NFTs, in English under `/nft-buy/` and in German under `/nft-kaufen/`.

- Podcast, all 66 episodes: [feed.theangelswing.art](https://feed.theangelswing.art/)
- The 3D gallery, still walkable: [oncyber.io/casperb_collab](https://oncyber.io/casperb_collab)

Everything is static: HTML, images and self-hosted fonts. The page makes no external requests, carries no tracking and needs no JavaScript. The article paths from the old site resolve here, and unknown paths land on `404.html`.

All rights to the artwork remain solely with the artists.
